## Phase 2 — Bull vs Bear Debate Digest

**Bull, round 1.** Q1 2026 just printed +85% revenue growth, the fastest in the company's history. U.S. commercial +133%. Adjusted EPS beat. **FY2026 guide raised by approximately $460M at the top.** Net dollar retention 139%. Net cash balance sheet. AIP is the most credible enterprise AI monetisation product in the public software set. Sentiment is two sided (healthy), price is below the 200 SMA (oversold on a multi year view), and the divergence between corrective YTD price and accelerating fundamentals is unsustainable. This is the textbook case for a fundamental long: best in class growth, strong balance sheet, secular tailwind, depressed price relative to fundamentals.

**Bear, round 1.** All of the above is true and the stock has still corrected YTD. The reason is forward P/S near 49x on the raised guide. Even if 2026 prints in line with the new guide ($7.66B), the stock at $147 is paying $147 today for $7.66B of one year forward sales — that is approximately 5x the multiple of a typical mature software peer. The closest historical analogues (SNOW, DDOG at 2021 peaks) saw 50% to 70% multiple compression over 24 months despite revenue continuing to grow. The bear case is not "Palantir's business is bad"; the bear case is "even a perfect Palantir compresses to a 25x to 30x P/S over 24 months, which means the stock can be flat to down on a great fundamental year". HSBC downgrade and "Look For Exit Point" commentary are the most credible institutional voices in the room and they are explicitly making this call.

**Bull, round 2.** Acknowledged: the SNOW/DDOG analogue is the strongest single bear data point. Two countervailing observations. First, **the muted reaction to the print is itself the de rating in real time** — the multiple compression is happening, and the equity is approximately flat YTD from Jan 1 to today, which means a portion of the historical analogue compression has already occurred. Second, the FY guide raise of approximately +6.4% above the prior top end on top of an 85% growth base implies operating leverage that the SNOW/DDOG comparables did not deliver at this growth rate; the right multiple for a name growing 85% with 139% NDR is structurally higher than for one growing 30% with 110% NDR. The valuation is rich, but it is not insanely rich by 2025 to 2026 standards once you adjust for the growth rate.

**Bear, round 2.** Acknowledged: the in flight de rating is real and the YTD chart is evidence the bear case has had partial execution already. The counter is that "partial de rating already done" is a far weaker claim than "de rating is finished". On the SNOW analogue, the 70% P/S compression happened over 24 months, not 24 weeks; the YTD correction in PLTR is approximately 19% from peak — that is the first year of the analogous compression at most. Forward P/S 49x is still 5x a mature peer. Strong fundamentals do not reset the clock on multiple compression; they just slow it. **Honest concession: PLTR can outperform the analogue if growth stays above 60% for two more quarters, in which case the multiple compression slows enough for fundamental compounding to drive returns. That is the bull's only path. Anything below 60% growth and the multiple finishes compressing on the SNOW timeline.**
